How it works

A decision,
not a list.

Focus reduces the cost of deciding what to do. It gathers, normalises, and ranks work — but keeps you in control of the final order.

1

Collect

Keep the original source visible. Work imported from email, calendar, and notes should never feel like it disappeared into a black box.

2

Explain

Show the reason: deadline, impact, dependency, energy, and the time currently available to you.

3

Focus

One best next step, a short queue, and an easy way to adjust or defer the recommendation — so Focus guides, not commands.

Features

Built around
intent.

Every component answers what it is, why it matters now, how long it takes, and what happens when focus begins.

📥

Unified Inbox

Email, calendar blocks, and note action items land in one queue — with real triage actions, not just "open in Gmail."

🔍

Notes Scanner

Paste from Apple Notes, Obsidian, Buildly Ink, or Gemini Notes. Focus extracts the tasks buried in your writing automatically.

🎯

Today View

One recommended task above the fold. A short queue beneath it. Everything else tucked away until you're ready.

⚡

Priority Reasons

"This unblocks two teammates" beats an opaque AI score. Plain-language explanations accompany every rank.

🧠

Learning Layer

Every time you deprioritise or dismiss a suggestion, Focus adjusts. Recommendations sharpen without configuration.

✏️

Quick Capture

Add any task before the thought disappears — from the tab bar, a widget, or a keyboard shortcut on Mac.
